# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Thai video editors and small editing teams who cut talking-head footage (interviews, client explainers, staff videos) into short vertical clips. They work on a Windows desktop, usually a 13 to 27 inch screen, in an office or at home, often for hours at a time. They are editors first, not developers: they know CapCut, timelines and subtitles, and they do not want to read JSON, logs or model names. Some team members only review work.

## Product Purpose

EditorKrab prepares an editable draft from long footage: transcript, story cuts, subtitles and a cover. The editor reviews it and finishes in CapCut or the local editor, then exports an MP4. Success is an editor who goes from a raw file to a reviewed draft without asking anyone what a screen means or which button comes next.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, plain-spoken. A good assistant editor who has the draft ready and tells you the one next step. Thai is the primary language; English is a full translation, not an afterthought.

## Anti-references

- Developer consoles and settings dumps: raw error text, JSON boxes, file paths as the main label.
- Mixed vocabulary for the same helper (AI, bot, Claude, Codex in one screen).
- Generic dark SaaS dashboards with gradient hero tiles and purple glow on everything.
- Hints that mention buttons that are not on the screen.

## Design Principles

1. One next step. Every screen makes the next action obvious and names it with the same words the button uses.
2. Speak editor, not engineer. Errors say what happened and what to do, in Thai first.
3. Footage first. Thumbnails, transcript and the preview carry the screen; chrome stays quiet.
4. Same control, same look. One button vocabulary, one set of tokens across every page.
5. Show only what this person can use. Team review tools appear for reviewers, not everyone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for text and controls on the dark theme. Every icon-only control has an accessible name. Visible keyboard focus. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Thai text needs looser line height than Latin (1.6 for body).
